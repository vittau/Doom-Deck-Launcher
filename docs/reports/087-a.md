## Compatibility decisions (evidence, not path inference)

- **Plutonia 2 is Plutonia.** `PL2.TXT` says `Game: The Plutonia Experiment`; `PL2INFO.TXT` says to
  use `PLUTONIA.WAD` and that `DOOM2.WAD` will cause errors. The `/idgames` `levels/doom2/...`
  path is not evidence for the IWAD. The `vanilla` alias resolves to 4 on Plutonia (D-030).
- **TNT: Revilution is TNT.** `TNTR.txt` says `Game: TNT`, `For Demos use TNT.wad`, and the release
  is a sequel to TNT: Evilution. The `vanilla` alias resolves to 4 on TNT.
- **Scythe II is Doom II**, stated in its release text. The author targets "any limit removing
  port"; the limit-removing convention gives `vanilla` (2 on Doom II).
- **The three spec 089 titles are Boom.** Going Down: Turbo carries a `COMPLVL` lump whose exact
  bytes are `boom`; The 10x10 Project and Speed of Doom declare Boom-compatible ports and have no
  `COMPLVL`, so the Boom convention gives `boom` (9). None may be inferred from the `/idgames`
  directory alone.
- **Embedded patches are not double-loaded.** Every WAD here carries a `DEHACKED` lump that
  DSDA-Doom applies automatically (`-noautoload` does not disable it). PL2 also ships an external
  `PL2.DEH` that differs from the embedded lump (MAP03 `Skull Island` vs `Emerald Eve`); TNTR ships
  `TNTR.deh`, a plain-DeHackEd encoding of the same data. Loading either external file after the
  embedded one would re-apply or overwrite the authors' values, so the recipes load only the WAD.
  The external files' sizes and hashes are recorded in the research for future reference but are
  not declared members/components.
- **ZDoom MAPINFO is inert.** DSDA-Doom removed ZDoom MAPINFO support in v0.30.0; PL2's bonus
  `MAP33`, TNT's map titles block, Going Down: Turbo's music block and Speed of Doom's episode
  presentation therefore do not control DSDA-Doom. UMAPINFO in Going Down: Turbo and The 10x10
  Project is supported and is the intended progression source. Each recipe has one required `main`
  component with one `maps` member at position 1 and no `dehacked` component.

## Product copy rewrite

Root and `main` component descriptions were rewritten as plain-English theme, map-count, campaign
and difficulty summaries. Engine names, `DEHACKED`, `MAPINFO`/`UMAPINFO`, `COMPLVL`, lump
inventories and source-port reasoning were removed from the product copy and remain in each
recipe's `[verification]` notes/limitations and in the research. The selected archives, package and
member identities, components, tags, compatibility and runtime suggestions are unchanged.

| Recipe | New root description |
| --- | --- |
| `scythe-ii` | A 32-map DOOM II campaign in six themed chapters, moving from castles and temples to tech bases and hellish gardens. The later maps are a serious difficulty step up, and the set has its own soundtrack and graphics. |
| `plutonia-2` | A 32-map sequel to The Plutonia Experiment that continues Plutonia's design and gameplay style across jungle, temple, base and hellish settings. It includes its own music and graphics. |
| `tnt-revilution` | A 32-map community sequel to TNT: Evilution that carries the fight from an Io thermal power station to a demonic fleet, with custom music, graphics and sounds. |
| `going-down-turbo` | A 32-map remaster of Going Down with faster, more forgiving combat and optional par times for 100% kills. Each map is one floor of a UAC tower descent, with the set's own soundtrack. |
| `the-10x10-project` | A DOOM II set with ten main maps, a secret map and a no-exit finale (twelve map slots). Each main map is built from only ten stock textures and ten stock flats, and the combat ranges from Plutonia-style to Sunder-esque. |
| `speed-of-doom` | A 33-map DOOM II campaign of fast, challenging combat in three difficulty chapters, ranging from small speedmap-style arenas to larger set-piece maps. It includes the bonus MAP33 'Descent to Nowhere' and its own soundtrack and graphics. |

campaign…", "All ten main maps, the secret map and the no-exit finale…"). A new offline assertion
rejects `DEHACKED`, `MAPINFO`, `COMPLVL`, `UMAPINFO`, lump, engine-family and `BEX` terms in this
copy and proves each title still carries at least one of those technical markers in verification.

## Artwork (R4): no compliant source, fallback kept

No `[[screenshots]]` entry or `crates/ddl/ui/assets/screenshots/<id>/` directory was added for
these titles, because no compliant in-game image could be obtained:

- DoomWiki returns Cloudflare HTTP 403 to automated requests, including the 10x10 `oldid=576470`
  page; the archived `Scythe_2` page shows only a title picture and two monster sprites, and the
  archived `Plutonia_2` page only a title picture — neither is the required recognisable in-game
  map view.
- Wayback then returned "Temporarily Offline"/connection resets, and the old official
  `rabotik.nl/pluto2.html` is HTTP 404. The remaining titles had no reachable candidate image page.
- Doomworld is Cloudflare-blocked for automation.

The existing painted fallback art remains. No image was synthesized, cropped from a title screen or
captured from a window. Full provenance of the attempts is in
`docs/research/reference-087-a.md` §8.
