## Metadata and progression correction (Vanguard, Lunatic)

An earlier draft called the bundled `MAPINFO` a Boom format and the `EMAPINFO` a ZDoom dialect.
That was wrong and is corrected everywhere (recipes, research, this report, tests):

- The `MAPINFO` lump in both releases is **ZDoom syntax** (`defaultmap`/`nocrouch`/`nojump` and
  `map MAP01 "…"` blocks with `titlepatch`/`next`/`music`). Boom has no `MAPINFO` lump.
- `EMAPINFO` in Lunatic is **Eternity's** metadata format.
- DSDA-Doom **v0.30.1 has no ZDoom MAPINFO reader**: `prboom2/src/dsda/mapinfo/` contains only
  `legacy.c`, `u.c` (UMAPINFO) and `hexen.c`. `legacy.c` is the **hardcoded** Doom/Doom II
  progression (`doom2_next[33]`, lines 128–135), not a lump parser.
- Therefore the author's custom titles and per-map music do not apply, and no `UMAPINFO`/`COMPLVL`
  overrides progression. After the last custom map the hardcoded table loads the **base IWAD's**
  next map: Vanguard MAP13 → stock MAP14, Lunatic MAP05 → stock MAP06. The player has to quit from
  the intermission to stop the run. The same fallback is documented for Arrival (MAP10 → stock
  MAP11) and Overboard (MAP14 → stock MAP15). Headless Chicken and MSCP do ship `UMAPINFO`, so
  their progression (episodes/`endcast`; per-map `next`/`endgame`) is the author's.
- `complevel boom` stays for both 2011 releases: the maps themselves use Boom generalized line
  types, regardless of the metadata lump dialect.
