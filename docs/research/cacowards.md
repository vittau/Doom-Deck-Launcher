# Cacowards inventory for the curated catalog (spec 042)

**Research date:** 2026-10-19 · **Scope:** every Doomworld Cacoward winner and runner-up, 2004–2025, plus the named special awards whose recipient is a mapset. No recipe is written by this task.

Labels: **Confirmed** = observed/quoted from a primary source in this session; **Proposed** = a DDL inference, not an upstream statement; **Needs Deck Validation** = cannot be checked on the macOS arm64 dev host.

## Method and evidence

- **Editions and pages.** Doomworld's own Cacowards index says the award series started in 2004; the index lists 2004–2025. Every edition was read from a raw Wayback `id_` snapshot because `doomworld.com` returns Cloudflare 403 to automation. Each table row links the exact page (`best1`…`best4`, `runnersup`, `others`, `multi`) and the table header gives the capture timestamp used. **Confirmed.**
- **Which entries count.** Top Ten/Twelve winners and every runner-up the edition published. 2004–2017 runners-up sit at the bottom of the `best*` pages; 2018–2020 they follow the winners; 2019 has a separate `runnersup` page; 2021+ runners-up carry the silver Cacoward and are on the same pages. Honorable Mentions are not winners and are excluded. **Confirmed.**
- **Special awards.** `others` and `multi` pages were read for named awards. Only awards whose recipient is a playable release (mapset, episode, megawad, deathmatch set, total conversion) are in the special-award table; Mapper/Creator of the Year, Codeaward, Dootaward, Best Gameplay Mod, Worst Wad entries that are not a single mapset, and feature columns are excluded and listed in the findings. **Confirmed.**
- **/idgames.** Paths and byte sizes come from the recursive index `https://www.gamers.org/pub/idgames/ls-laR.gz` (fetched 2026-10-19, 43,741 entries). Numeric `?id=` links on old Cacowards pages were resolved through Wayback snapshots of the file page, so the path is the one Doomworld itself recorded. `.txt` sidecars were fetched from the same mirror to read `Title`, `Game`/`Base`, `Advanced engine needed`, `Primary purpose` and any stated complevel. **Confirmed** for each quoted field. **Nothing downloaded was executed or extracted.**
- **DDL state.** `catalog/*.toml` on `main` (89 recipes, including the spec 037 Doomer Boards set). A recipe is marked as a match only when its normalized title equals the award title (with the explicit aliases BTSX E1/E2 and Eviternity II). Tag/citation status comes from the recipe's `tags` and its `[verification]` text. **Confirmed.**
- **Verdict rule.** *Playable* only when the text names a DSDA-Doom-compatible family (vanilla, limit-removing, Boom, MBF, MBF21); *not playable* when it names a port DSDA-Doom does not implement (GZDoom/ZDoom/Zandronum/Odamex/EDGE/Eternity/UDMF/ZScript/Doom 3); *unknown* otherwise. No complevel is guessed: where the text does not state one, the table does not invent it. Every verdict still needs Deck gameplay validation.

## Findings

1. **There is no 1994 Cacowards edition.** The official archive says "Since 2004, the year following 10 years of Doom". A CDX search for `doomworld.com/cacowards/1994*` returns no captures. The 1994–2003 period is covered by *5 Years of Doom* (1998) and *10 Years of Doom* (2003) top-WAD lists, which are not Cacowards. The inventory therefore starts at **2004**; if the coordinator wants the retrospective top-100 lists too, that is separate scope. **Confirmed.**
2. **The award list is stable in shape but not in size.** 2004–2020 published a Top Ten; 2021 and 2023–2025 published a Top Twelve; 2022 returned to a Top Ten. 2016 published eleven Top Ten entries (observed on the page, not a parsing artefact). Runner-ups exist every year except 2011, which has none. **Confirmed.**
3. **Special awards are a moving target.** Mordeth Award and Mockaward run through most of the period; Best DM/Multiplayer is a mapset award in most years; Best Gameplay Mod, Codeaward, Machaward, Dootaward and Mapper/Creator of the Year are not mapsets. From 2018 the special-award page is mostly feature columns, so only Mordeth (and occasional Machaward mapset winners) remain in scope. **Confirmed.**
4. **Availability is good but not automatic.** Of the 380 Top Ten/Twelve + runner-up entries, 278 have a verified `/idgames` path in this pass. The remainder are contemporary releases published only on forums/itch/ModDB, commercial or console-only releases, or old entries whose archive filename does not match the award title; the table says `not matched`, never `not on /idgames`. **Confirmed** for the matches; **Proposed** for the reason on each unmatched row.
5. **Port fit splits the list.** 181 of the 380 are *playable* from the quoted evidence, 62 are *not playable* (GZDoom/ZDoom/Zandronum/Doom 3/standalone), and 137 are *unknown* (mostly older text files that do not state an engine). The playable share rises sharply after 2012 as pages start printing a port/format line. **Confirmed** for the quotes; **Needs Deck Validation** for play.
6. **DDL coverage is narrow.** 10 recipes match an award entry: 9 carry `cacowards` and 1 does not. **`going-down` (2014 Top Ten) is missing the tag and its notes say the category was unconfirmed; this page settles it.** AUGER;ZENITH is the only Doomer Boards recipe with a Cacoward (2021 runner-up). **Confirmed.**

Column notes: `/idgames` is the archive path on the mirror (`not matched` means no verified archive); *Size* is the archive size from `ls-laR.gz`; *IWAD* is Confirmed when the text says it and Proposed when inferred from the archive directory; *Port / format* quotes the text file or the Cacowards line; *DSDA-Doom* is the compatibility verdict above.
