Identity & type

The Rose Compass (Stained Glass) identity and Ember Cathedral scene are original DDL artwork, licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. The identity guide records their authorship and exports. The scene and social preview are existing project exports; no original DOOM title artwork is used on this page.

Launcher previews

Home, Discover, Play setup, mapset detail and Audio settings are current 1280×800 off-screen renders of the real Slint UI with sample state, shared with the README. They illustrate the source tree, not physical Steam Deck testing. Screenshot generation is documented in the rendering script.

Embedded mapset imagery belongs to its respective authors. It is reused within the launcher previews under the project’s existing D-024 screenshot convention, not relicensed as DDL artwork.
